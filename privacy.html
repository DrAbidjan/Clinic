Privacy & security

Patient data stays yours

How ClinicRecords.pk keeps records separate and protected.

Your records belong to your clinic

Patient records entered in ClinicRecords.pk belong to the clinic or hospital that entered them.

How they are protected

  • Each clinic sees only its own patients. The database enforces this itself, not just the screen.
  • Everyone logs in. Dentists, accountants and admins each have their own login and role.
  • X-rays and photos are stored privately and are opened only through your clinic's login.
  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS) between your device and the database.
  • Earnings are PIN protected and visible to the clinic admin only.
  • Medical alerts are not printed on prescriptions given to patients.

What we ask of you

  • Use a strong password and do not share logins.
  • Log out on shared computers.
  • Keep a backup of important records.
Questions about privacy or about how your data is kept? Ask me directly – I will answer honestly.
